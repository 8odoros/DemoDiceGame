Click on dice to select them for re-rolling. You get up to 4 rolls per turn.

Scoring: 1s→red gems | 2s→blue gems (pairs) | 3s→green gems (triplets) | 4s→purple gems (quartets) or black cubes | 5s→blue gem (any) | 6s→progressive rewards

Cards: After both players finish their turns, there's a shopping phase where players can purchase cards with their newly earned gems. Each player has 3 active card slots. When slots are full, new cards replace old ones, which go to your scoring pile. Only cards in your scoring pile count for final points!
